How to read this page

Your score is the % of your required tasks done on time. Example: 38 of 40 tasks done = 95%. It is a %, so trainers with more classes are not punished.

Excellent 95%+Well done. Keep it up.
Good 85–94%Fix your missed items by Wednesday.
Needs improvement 70–84%Fix by Wednesday + 10-min check-in with Kian.
Action plan below 70%Private meeting and written improvement plan.
No sessions loggedYou taught but didn't log. Log every session in the teacher console.

What we check, and what to do if you miss one:

    Unassigned = student work with no logged session, so no trainer gets credit. Log every session to fix this.

    Think a result is wrong? Tell Thiri by Monday with proof (screenshot or link). If you're right, it gets corrected.

    Not tracked here yet: weekly random videos, showcase and group videos in Lark, and parent meetings. Thiri checks those in the weekly sheet.
